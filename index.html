CLIMATE MODELLING · DATA ANALYSIS

Evaluating Earth-System Model Performance in Capturing Sea-Surface Temperature across Australia's Great Barrier Reef

Project purpose

To evaluate how well climate models simulate sea surface temperature across the Great Barrier Reef and investigate potential future changes under different climate scenarios.

My contributions

  • Analysed observed sea surface temperature data and CMIP6 climate model outputs using R.
  • Evaluated model performance using statistical metrics.
  • Compared future climate scenarios.
  • Prepared a scientific research report and presented a research poster.
02✳

BIODIVERSITY · FIELD RESEARCH

Toohey Forest BioBlitz: Biodiversity Observation and Reporting

Project purpose

To observe and document biodiversity in Toohey Forest, Brisbane, Australia.

My contributions

  • Participated in approximately eight hours of field observations.
  • Recorded 131 observations covering 71 species using iNaturalist.
  • Documented plants, insects, fungi, and birds.
  • Prepared a written report covering fieldwork observations, challenges, SWOT analysis, and personal reflections.
